# Agentic AI, Explained

A static, hand-drawn interactive map of agentic AI, published with GitHub Pages. There is no build step and no package manager.

## Layout

- `v1-alpha/`, … — one self-contained site per version. Old versions stay online unchanged. The root `index.html` forwards to the first version in `versions.js` and keeps the `#hash` (**every new version is added at the top of `versions.js` in the same PR**, which moves the redirect and fills the version dropdown; `scripts/root-redirect.test.js` fails otherwise), except that v1-alpha deep links (`#block-…`, `#zone-…`, `#link-…`, `#step-…`, v1 guide names) stay on v1-alpha.
- `v3-alpha/` — a guided path instead of one map. Frozen: it stays as it is. Its content lives in `assets/js/data/` (`core.js`, `entries.js`, `sources.js`, `guides.js`; no `data.js`) and `node scripts/check-v3.js` checks it. `scripts/validate-data.js` covers the versions that have a `data.js`.
- `v4-alpha/` — v3 plus its successors: every term and guide has its own page (`v4-alpha/terms/<id>/`, `v4-alpha/guides/<id>/`, generated from `v4-alpha/index.html` by `node scripts/build-v4-pages.js`; run it after changing the term or guide list or the shell in `index.html`, `check-v4.js` fails while they are stale), a rail of dots shows where you are, and everything is told twice, in a kitchen voice and a technical voice (`<html data-mode>`, `?view=`). Content: `assets/js/data/` (`core.js`, `entries.js`, `sources.js`, `guides.js` and the `voice-*.js` files). The kitchen voice may not use technical words outside a bold bridge, the technical voice no kitchen picture (`scripts/check-voice.js`). `node scripts/check-v4.js` checks it.
- `v5-alpha/` — v4-alpha with the two voices side by side in every section instead of behind a switch (kitchen left, technical right, stacked below 900px; no `data-mode`, no `?view=`). Same content files as v4; `voice.js` hands the views `voice.kitchen` and `voice.tech`. Its pages are generated with `node scripts/build-v5-pages.js` and checked with `node scripts/check-v5.js`. The root redirect moved on to v6-alpha.
- `v6-alpha/` — v5-alpha with the diagram in the middle: every chapter, every term page, the hero and every idea of the recap is three columns (kitchen left, map or picture in the middle, technical right), and `assets/js/arrows.js` draws an arrow from the passage in view in each outer column to the pieces of the map it is about (`AE3.points` in `core.js`: one list per paragraph and one for the example, per voice; `check-v6.js` verifies it). What both voices say alike is told once in the middle (the chapter's question is the technical `problem`, the kitchen voice has none). The map is `layout: "spine"` in `map.js`: the open chapter plus every other piece folded to its heading. Wide and tall screens (1300px, 620px) get three columns and arrows; below that the map sits above the two voices, on a phone everything stacks. Pages are generated with `node scripts/build-v6-pages.js`, content is checked with `node scripts/check-v6.js`.
- `v7-alpha/` — a different kind of version: a hand-drawn, animated comic of the kitchen story (nine pages, one per chapter plus a cover and a finale; each page is a grid of panels that arrive one by one with Next, Back, the arrow keys, a swipe or Play). It has no `data.js`, no term or guide pages and no generated pages. `assets/js/draw.js` is the pen (shapes to SVG through rough.js), `props.js` the drawings (people, furniture, things; the header comment lists them), `comic.js` the reader, and `assets/js/data/page-<n>.js` one page of panels each (`props-extra-*.js` hold extra drawings). Kitchen voice only; the strip "behind the pictures" at the end of each page names the real terms and links to the v6 term pages. Deep links: `#ch-1` … `#ch-8`, `#ch-e`, `#map` (the end), and `#e-<id>` (the page whose strip has that term). Content is checked with `node scripts/check-v7.js`.
- `v8-alpha/` — v7-alpha's comic as one continuous strip that grows from left to right like a storyline, instead of separate pages. It starts as a cover card; each step (Next, the arrow keys, Space, a click on the dashed ghost of the next item, or Play) adds the next item at the right end (a chapter title card, then its panels, then the strip "The real names") and the camera slides along to it. The strip can also be scrolled sideways (trackpad, wheel, mouse drag, touch). Same drawings and chapter content as v7 (`draw.js`, `props.js`, `assets/js/data/page-<n>.js`, one file per chapter), a new reader in `comic.js` and a new layout in `style.css`. Kitchen voice only; each chapter ends with the strip that names the real terms and links to the v6 term pages. Deep links: `#ch-1` … `#ch-8` (the strip shows everything up to the end of that chapter and slides to its title card), `#ch-e`, `#map` (the end), and `#e-<id>`. Content is checked with `node scripts/check-v8.js`.
- `v9-alpha/` — the kitchen story on one single screen, no scrolling: the kitchen builds itself piece by piece (a list of things in `assets/js/data/kitchen.js`, a list of steps in `data/story.js` that show, hide, move and pose them), the chef walks to where the next thing happens, and in the last chapter the real names (`data/names.js`, each a v6 term) are laid over the faded kitchen with dashed outlines and clickable labels. Click, keys, swipe, a scrubber, or Play with a pause of 1, 2 or 3 s (speed button). Reuses `draw.js` (plus `actor`, `halo`, `room`), `props.js` and `props-extra-*.js` of v8; `props-v9.js` adds drawings; `assets/js/kitchen.js` is the reader. Kitchen voice in the story, technical voice in the last chapter. Deep links: `#ch-1` … `#ch-8`, `#ch-e`, `#map`, `#e-<id>`. Content is checked with `node scripts/check-v9.js`.
- `v10-alpha/` — v9's kitchen and v4's reference in one version. `index.html` is the v9 kitchen (the story, the way in for a beginner); `dive/index.html` is v4's start page (chapters, map, recap) and the shell of the generated pages `terms/<id>/` and `guides/<id>/` (`node scripts/build-v10-pages.js`; `check-v10.js` fails while they are stale). It has two stylesheets with the same tokens (`assets/css/kitchen.css` for the story, `style.css` for the deep dive) and `places.css` for what ties them together: the two tabs in the top bar (Story, Deep dive), the card of every real name with a button to its term page, bold words in story captions that link to their term (`data/bridges.js`), and "Where in the kitchen" on every term page (`assets/js/locator.js` draws the kitchen from `data/kitchen.js`, `story.js` and `names.js`). The 21 names of the kitchen are the 21 terms of the deep dive, in the same chapters. The deep dive's technical voice is the default (key `ae10-mode`). Deep links: the story's `#ch-1` … `#ch-8`, `#ch-e`, `#map`, `#e-<id>`; the deep dive's `dive/#ch-…`, `dive/#map`, `terms/<id>/`, `guides/<id>/`. Content is checked with `node scripts/check-v10.js`.
- `versions.js`, `version-switcher.js`, `version-switcher.css` (repo root) — the one list of versions (newest first, each with a note and two flags: `guided` has `#ch-N` chapters, `pages` has term and guide pages) and the dropdown in the header of every version that reads it. Every version's `index.html` loads `../versions.js` and `../version-switcher.js` before `</body>` (the generated term and guide pages get the paths from `build-v4-pages.js`). These are the only files in the older version folders that may change after release, and only for the dropdown.
- `<version>/assets/js/data.js` — **all content**: zones, connectors, blocks, walkthrough steps, story, guides, `SOURCES` (links per block) and `FACTS` (dated status notes per block).
- `<version>/assets/js/app.js` — rendering and interaction (plain ES5-style JS in an IIFE, no frameworks). `style.css` holds the layout and themes.
- `<version>/DESIGN.md` — the design system: tokens, shared classes (`.btn`, `.eyebrow`, `.badge`, `.card`, `.zdot`…) and naming rules. Use tokens, not raw values, in `style.css`.
- `assets/vendor/` (rough.js) and `assets/fonts/` are third-party files with their licences. Never edit them.
- `.github/workflows/pages.yml` deploys the whole repository root on every push to `main`.

## Content rules (data.js)

- A block needs `name`, `label`, `short`, `eli5` (the restaurant-kitchen picture), `what`, `when`, `avoid`, `sota`, `examples` and `related`. A new block must also go into a zone's `blocks` (or `groups`) list.
- Inline markup in strings: `` `code` ``, `**bold**`, `[[block-id]]` or `[[block-id|label]]`, and `[text](https://…)`. Nothing else is parsed.
- Write dated facts with a month and year ("(October 2026)"). The site is a snapshot: `meta.updated` / `meta.snapshot`.
- Sources: primary sources first (vendor docs, specs, standards bodies, regulators), then seminal papers (arXiv or publisher page), then videos from large, reputable channels only. HTTPS only.
- Voice: plain and short. British spelling where the site already uses it ("licence", "organised").

## Hard constraints

- **No external requests.** No CDNs, web fonts, analytics or embeds. Self-host everything under `assets/`; videos are plain links. A PreToolUse hook blocks edits that load remote resources.
- Accessibility promises: works with the keyboard alone (`/` focuses search, `Esc` closes; in v1, arrow keys step through the walkthrough and story), respects reduced motion, light and dark themes.
- Deep links are public URLs: v1's `#block-<id>`, `#zone-<id>`, `#link-<id>`, `#step-<n>`, `#<guide>` and v3's, v4's, v5's, v6's, v7's, v8's, v9's and v10's `#ch-<n>`, `#ch-e`, `#map`, `#e-<id>`, `#guide-<id>`. Don't rename ids without a reason.

## Privacy (public repository)

- Never put personal information in source files, docs, commit messages or PR titles and bodies: no real names or emails, no local paths (`C:\Users\…`, `/home/…`, scratch folders with a user name), no machine or user names.
- Never link to personal or internal places: no claude.ai session or project links, no private tools. Public source citations on the site are content and stay.
- So leave out the `Claude-Session:` trailer and any session link in commits and PR bodies; a plain `Co-Authored-By` line is fine. Don't add a "Requested by <name>" line or a project-thread link to PR bodies.
- Before opening a PR, run `git diff origin/main -- . ':!CLAUDE.md' | grep -nEi 'claude\.ai|C:\\|/Users/|/home/|@[a-z]+\.'` and clean up what it finds.

## Pull requests

- Body structure: a short summary of what changed, then a "Details" list. Nothing else.
- No "Generated with Claude Code" line, no other tool or session attribution, in the body.

## Commands

- Validate content: `node scripts/validate-data.js [version]`. A PostToolUse hook runs it after every edit to `data.js`, and `node --check` after any JS edit.
- Validate v3 content: `node scripts/check-v3.js`; v4 content: `node scripts/check-v4.js`; v5 content: `node scripts/check-v5.js`; v6 content: `node scripts/check-v6.js`; v7 content: `node scripts/check-v7.js`; v8 content: `node scripts/check-v8.js`; v9 content: `node scripts/check-v9.js`; v10 content: `node scripts/check-v10.js` (tests: `node --test scripts/*.test.js`, which also fails when the root `index.html` does not forward to the newest version folder). While writing a voice file, `node scripts/check-voice.js <file>` lints just that file.
- Preview: the `site` entry in `.claude/launch.json` (`python -m http.server 8000`), then open `/v1-alpha/`; the `site-v3` entry serves the same root on port 8001 (open `/v3-alpha/`, `/v4-alpha/`, `/v5-alpha/`, `/v6-alpha/`, `/v7-alpha/`, `/v8-alpha/`, `/v9-alpha/` or `/v10-alpha/`), so two sessions can preview at once.
- `/check-links` checks the links (and `/check-links stale` lists aging facts). `/new-version <name>` cuts a release.
- Agents: `content-fact-checker` (per block or zone), `a11y-reviewer` (browser audit).
